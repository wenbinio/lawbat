Redact identifiers

Deterministic detection of emails, card numbers, IBANs, IDs, phones, IPs and URLs — regex + checksums, no AI. You review each hit; only the ones you keep are removed.

Review

Recall-first: every detected identifier is pre-selected — better to over-redact and untick than to miss one. high = checksum-validated; review = heuristic (esp. names/orgs, which over-flag on purpose) — scan these and untick false positives before applying.

Redacted output

The identifier text is removed from the output (not just visually masked).

Honest framing. Detection is reasonable-efforts and deterministic — it is never "safe to send." Structured identifiers (email, card, IBAN, NRIC) are caught reliably; names & organisations are heuristic — caught only when Title-Cased/cued, and reliable free-form coverage is the later statistical/NER tier, NOT this deterministic pass. The lawyer owns the residual-identification judgment. This browser demo handles plain-text formats (.txt .md .eml .csv .json); the full product also extracts PDF/DOCX/XLSX/PPTX and OCRs scanned PDFs, all locally.
