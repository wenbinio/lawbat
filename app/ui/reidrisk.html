Re-identification risk

How to read this estimate
⚠
k is an ESTIMATE under an independence assumption — an OPTIMISTIC lower bound on uniqueness that may UNDER-STATE risk.

It assumes quasi-identifiers are independent, but real ones are correlated, so the true k is usually LOWER (more re-identifiable) than shown. This is NOT a safety verdict; it is never “safe to send.” It can only ever RAISE concern, never clear a document.

⚕
Medical conditions are TIERED only — common (weak signal) / rare (strong signal).

A precise k is never computed from crude national prevalence (it under-counts small-cell uniqueness, the wrong direction), so a medical condition is surfaced and tiered but never priced into the k product.
